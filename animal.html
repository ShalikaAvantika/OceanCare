Lekang Turtle

Lepidochelys olivacea

A species of turtle that lives in shallow tropical and subtropical waters. Loggerhead turtles are found nesting in the Indian Ocean, the Pacific Ocean, and on the coasts of large islands.

Good Impact

Penyu lekang memakan ubur-ubur, moluska, kepiting, dan organisme laut lain sehingga membantu mengontrol populasi mangsa tertentu

Bad Impact

Turtle eggs and meat are often hunted illegally, threatening their population

North Pacific Gray Whale

Eubalaena japonica

The North Pacific right whale is a really rare and endangered baleen whale

Good Impact

Whale poop is nutrient-rich, fertilizing the waters and boosting the productivity of phytoplankton, which forms the base of the marine food chain.

Bad Impact

Disrupting the availability of zooplankton, especially in the Bering Sea, thus reducing their main food source

Albatros Laysan (Seabirds)

Phoebastria immutabilis

Albatrosses are basically wild animals that live far out in the open ocean and rarely interact directly with human settlements

Good Impact

Laysan hunts squid, fish, and small crustaceans. This activity helps keep the marine ecosystem balanced by controlling the number of certain prey.

Bad Impact

Disrupting the availability of zooplankton, especially in the Bering Sea, thus reducing their main food source
