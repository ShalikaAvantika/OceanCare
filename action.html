Concrete Actions for Ocean Care

Small actions can help protect marine animals and their habitats.

Reduce Single-Use Plastic

Reduce plastic waste by:

  • Using a reusable water bottle
  • Bringing a reusable shopping bag
  • Avoiding single-use straws
Daily Steps

Support Ocean Conservation

Help protect marine life by:

  • Donating to conservation organizations
  • Volunteering in beach clean-ups
  • Sharing ocean conservation information
Education & Action

Keep the Beach Clean

Keep beaches clean by:

  • Taking your trash home
  • Joining local beach clean-ups
  • Never leaving cigarette butts on the sand
Community Action

🌊 Did you know?

More than 8 million tonnes of plastic are estimated to enter the oceans every year. Plastic pollution can harm marine animals and ecosystems. Small changes in our daily habits can make a difference.
