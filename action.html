Concrete Actions for Ocean Care

The small steps we take today can save hundreds of marine species from extinction.

Reduce Single-Use Plastic

Switch your everyday plastic stuff for eco-friendly options:

  • Use a refillable water bottle (tumbler)
  • Bring your own fabric shopping bag
  • Use a stainless steel or bamboo straw
Daily Steps

Supporting Ocean Conservation

Help the ecosystem and marine life rescue program:

  • Donating to a marine conservation organization
  • Join the beach education volunteer program
  • Spread awareness on social media
Education & Action

Keeping the Beach Clean

Prevent trash from entering the open sea:

  • Always take your own trash home
  • Joining a *local beach* clean-up
  • Refuse to throw cigarette butts in the sand
