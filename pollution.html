Ocean Pollution

Learn about the types of ocean pollution and their effects on the environment.

What Is Ocean Pollution?

Ocean pollution occurs when harmful substances, such as waste, plastic litter, and chemicals, enter the ocean due to human activities. About 80% of ocean pollution comes from land-based sources.

Types of Ocean Pollution

Plastic Waste

Bottles, plastic bags, and straws pollute the water and can be swallowed by marine animals.

Chemical Waste

Toxic substances from factories and pesticides can enter rivers and eventually flow into the ocean.

Oil Spills

Oil leaks from tankers can spread across the water's surface and damage marine ecosystems.
